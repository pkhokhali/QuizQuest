# Privacy Policy for QuizQuest

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026

Welcome to **QuizQuest** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe, transparent, and responsible manner.

This Privacy Policy explains how QuizQuest collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By downloading, installing, or using QuizQuest, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Credentials:** When you register or sign in, we collect your authentication credentials (such as your phone number, email address, and encrypted password managed securely via Google Firebase Authentication).
- **Profile Information:** We collect your chosen display name (or nickname), grade level (if provided), avatar preferences, and preferred language (English or Nepali).
- **School or Classroom Code (Optional):** If you choose to connect with your school or study circle, we process the school code you enter to associate your profile with your educational group.

### B. Gameplay & Performance Data
- **Quiz Responses & History:** We record your quiz answers, time spent per question, daily quiz completions, Revenge Round progress, and live 1v1 battle match results.
- **Progress & Gamification:** We store your earned Experience Points (XP), current level, streak count, Olympic leaderboard ranking, and unlocked trophies/awards.

### C. Automatically Collected Information
- **Device & Technical Data:** We may collect non-identifying technical data including your operating system version, app version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status to ensure reliable app operation.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** If you grant notification permissions, we collect an Expo Push Token to send you daily quest reminders, streak protection notices, and friend battle invitations. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:
1. **Provide and Maintain the Service:** Deliver daily quizzes, calculate scores, maintain leaderboards, orchestrate real-time 1v1 trivia battles, and update user achievements.
2. **Personalize Your Experience:** Remember your preferred language (English or Nepali), avatar, theme preferences, and customized grade-appropriate questions.
3. **Prevent Cheating and Ensure Fair Play:** Ensure fair play across leaderboards and multiplayer challenges.
4. **Service Reliability & Customer Support:** Monitor server performance, diagnose bugs, and respond to user inquiries or support requests.
5. **Educational Engagement:** Help schools and teachers organize student trivia circles when school codes are utilized.

**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers.**

To provide a secure and reliable experience, QuizQuest integrates with reputable third-party infrastructure providers that process data strictly on our behalf:

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Educational Safeguards)

QuizQuest is designed for a general audience, including students and young learners. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and applicable student data privacy regulations:
- We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 beyond what is necessary to participate in educational quizzes (e.g., username/nickname and avatar).
- We do not require real names, physical addresses, or location tracking.
- We do not show personalized targeted advertising.
- Parents or legal guardians can review, edit, or request deletion of their child's account and data at any time by contacting us at **support@quizquest.app** (or **prabinkhokhali89@gmail.com**).

## 5. Data Retention and Security

- **Security Measures:** We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data transmission and secure password hashing via Firebase. Database access is restricted to authorized backend services.
- **Data Retention:** We retain your account data and gameplay history as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your personal information is permanently removed from our active databases.

---

## 6. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:
1. **In-App:** Go to the **Profile** tab in the QuizQuest app and tap **Delete Account** (or request deletion via profile settings).
2. **Via Email:** Send an email to **prabinkhokhali89@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"* and your registered phone number or email address. We will permanently delete your account and all associated gameplay history within 7 business days.
